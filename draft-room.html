VONA = a player's projected points minus the best player at his position you'd expect to still be available at your next pick (top-ADP players assumed gone in between). It re-prices scarcity live: pass on a cliffing position and VONA drops; deep positions stay flat. Built on PFF projections + ESPN ADP; projections compress the middle, so trust the top-tier cliffs most.
